Controller v3 · PPO-first recovery plan

Executed PPO v3 reward and training sweep

This pre-run plan has been executed as a joint-only exploratory study. The implementation repaired episode observability, swept PPO and reward details, and added a matched three-budget diagnostic. Spatial-only, temporal-only, and MPC controllers were not trained.

Final outcome: US and Global each produced only 1/10 safe selected seeds; see output/ppo_v3_reward_sweep/results_report.md
Historical-plan boundary: the escalation branches below document options considered before execution. They were not activated. The canonical protocol, exact results, and claim boundary are in output/ppo_v3_reward_sweep/.

01Evidence that motivates this sweep

31/40Joint seeds failed the 99.99% batch-completion floor.
0.95⁵⁴=6%GAE credit remaining at the longest 270-minute deadline.
KL≈0.028Late catastrophic-seed update, with clip fraction ≈0.35.
×10⁴ scale gapSpatial reward used raw dollars; completion-guarded joint mode used 1e−4 scaling.
Hidden horizon

No episode progress in state

The policy pays for terminal pool but cannot observe that the month is ending. Hour-of-day repeats every 288 steps.

Long-horizon credit

GAE λ=.95 is short for several cells

Deadlines span 8–54 steps. λ=.99 retains 58–92% credit across that range; λ=.95 retains only 6–66%.

What is not broken

SB3 defaults already cover several tweaks

Installed SB3 2.9 already uses orthogonal init, separate policy/value MLPs, normalized advantages, entropy=0, and no value clipping.

Internet consensus, applied carefully: SB3 recommends normalized inputs and tuned hyperparameters for custom continuous tasks; implementation-detail studies emphasize reward scaling, GAE, learning-rate schedules, and KL behavior. Local measurements show observations are moderate (worst max 13.4), so observation normalization is a candidate—not the assumed root cause.

36 short runs

6 candidates × 2 joint configs × 3 seeds × 150k steps. At prior throughput, roughly 20–35 minutes wall clock with 16 workers.

Promotion

Safety is absolute

Require service completion 1.0, batch ≥0.9999, zero expiry, and no service-backlog outlier. Then rank worst-seed and mean cost.

Control

It measures the effect of making terminal/deadline state observable before credit or reward changes.

07Kill gates and algorithm escalation

Round 1 · 3 seedsEliminate any candidate with service loss, batch <99.99%, expiry, or catastrophic backlog.
Round 2 · 5 seedsTop reward/training variants must be safe in every seed and improve worst-seed cost.
Full dev · 10 seedsJoint must beat safe spatial with a positive paired CI on a–d development.
Escalate onceIf not, temporal-only diagnostic; then safety/MPC. RecurrentPPO/SAC only after feasibility.
Cost control: worst case before MPC is about 36 short runs + 40 medium runs + 20 full runs, but successive halving should consume the equivalent of roughly 25–35 full-budget runs—not another blind 80-model campaign.
Evidence boundary: v3 is post-hoc. Tune only on a–d development data; freeze once; evaluate e–h once. Report the follow-up as exploratory because e–h was already exposed by v2.

08Required tests and diagnostics

Unit
  • Progress reaches 0→1 exactly.
  • Deadline buckets conserve pool demand.
  • Idle subtraction changes returns by a policy-independent constant.
  • Potential shaping telescopes to a constant.
  • Penalty candidates exceed recomputed floors.
Training
  • Log KL, clip fraction, explained variance, value loss, entropy/std.
  • Log service backlog, completion, terminal pool every rollout.
  • Save/restore VecNormalize statistics.
  • Flag action saturation at ±3.
  • Abort unsafe candidate early.
Evaluation
  • Full-dollar objective unchanged.
  • Deterministic held-out evaluation.
  • Paired joint-vs-spatial seed statistics.
  • Demand charge and 1h/3h ramps remain independent.
  • All candidate/protocol/model hashes recorded.
